Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

FiNARTHiS Academy ("we", "us") runs trading-education masterclasses and courses. This policy explains what personal details we collect when you register, why we collect them, and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

In short: we collect only what we need to send you the masterclass and related course information. We never sell your details, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

1. What we collect

When you register on our website or through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number, and a WhatsApp number if it is different
  • Your email address
  • The masterclass session you chose
  • Your consent to be contacted, and the date and time you gave it
  • The ad or link you came from (for example, "facebook" or the campaign name), so we know which ads work

We do not ask for your PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, trading account details, passwords or any information about your trades. If someone claiming to be from FiNARTHiS asks for these, do not share them.

2. Why we use it

PurposeWhat we do
Masterclass accessSend you the joining link, reminders and the free guide on WhatsApp, SMS or email.
Course informationTell you about our paid courses, cohorts and setup help related to the masterclass. You can stop these at any time.
SupportReply when you message or call us.
Improving our adsCount how many people registered from each ad, using Meta Pixel and Google tags on our website.

3. Cookies and ad measurement

Our website may use the Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags. These tell Meta and Google that a registration happened, so they can measure our ads. They may use cookies on your browser. You can control ad cookies in your browser settings and in your Facebook and Google ad preferences.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your details. We use these services to run the masterclass, and they store data for us:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Drive) to store registrations and host the guide
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) for lead forms, ads and messages
  • Video meeting provider (such as Zoom or Google Meet) to run the live session
  • GitHub Pages to host this website

We may also share details if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your registration details for up to 12 months after your last contact with us, then delete them. If you join a paid course, we keep the records needed for billing and tax as the law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: reply STOP on WhatsApp, or email us.
  • See, correct or delete your details: email us and we will act within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: you can do this at any time. It does not affect messages already sent.
  • Complain: contact us first. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Keeping it safe

Registrations are stored in a private Google account with two-step verification. Only the FiNARTHiS team can see them.

8. Education only

FiNARTHiS Academy provides trading education. We do not give buy or sell recommendations or investment advice, and we do not promise returns. NISM certification is a qualification, not a SEBI registration. FiNARTHiS is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst.
