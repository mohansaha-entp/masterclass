Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what FiNARTHiS Academy collects when you register for our free masterclass at finarthis.in, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name, contact number, WhatsApp number (if different) and email address.

How we use it

  • To confirm your registration and send you the free guide.

We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Where it is stored

Registrations are saved in a private Google Sheet that only FiNARTHiS Academy can access. Messages are sent through WhatsApp from +91 89809 08278.

Advertising and analytics

This website uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits and registrations that come from our ads on Facebook and Instagram. When you register, the Pixel may send Meta an encrypted (hashed) form of contact details, so Meta can count the registration without receiving them in readable form. You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you remain on our list, and delete them within 30 days of a request.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to info@finarthis.in or messaging WhatsApp +91 89809 08278.

Education only

FiNARTHiS Academy provides trading education only. We do not provide investment advice, research recommendations or trading signals, and we are not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst.
