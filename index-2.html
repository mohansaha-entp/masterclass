    Trading is math. Here's how.

    01

    Your edge is a number.

    Edge per trade = (Win rate × Average win) − (Loss rate × Average loss) − Costs

    Example (illustration only): Win 40% at ₹1,500, lose 60% at ₹800, costs ₹150 per trade →

    0.40 × 1,500 − 0.60 × 800 − 150 = −₹30 per trade

    Feels like a good system. Still loses money. You can only fix what you measure.

    02

    Position size is a formula, not a feeling.

    Quantity = Money you can afford to lose on this trade ÷ Distance to stop-loss

    Example: ₹5,000 risk ÷ ₹5 stop distance = 1,000 shares.

    Same risk on every trade. No more one big loss wiping out ten wins.

    03

    A daily loss limit is a rule, not hope.

    If today's loss ≥ maximum loss per day → no more trades today

    One bad morning stays one bad morning.

    All numbers are illustrations for learning, not results or recommendations.
